# About Me

Hello! I am **Kangwei Zhong (钟康维)**, a Ph.D. student in the [System Software and Security Laboratory](https://security.fudan.edu.cn/) at [Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/). I am advised by Prof. [Zhemin Yang](https://yangzhemin.github.io/) and Prof. [Min Yang](https://min-yang-fudan.github.io/).

My research interests include **agent security** and **mobile security**. I am broadly interested in understanding and mitigating security risks in LLM-based agents, mobile applications, and app-in-app ecosystems.

Please feel free to contact me at [kwzhong23@m.fudan.edu.cn](mailto:kwzhong23@m.fudan.edu.cn).

# Research Interests

- Agent Security
- Mobile Security
- Vulnerability Discovery and Security Analysis
